Every episode of [[youtube-video-series]] must include comedy, not only a magical adventure with a lesson: at least 3 memorable funny moments, playful banter, family-friendly visual comedy (exaggerated faces, surprises, harmless slapstick), and one standout comedy scene viewers will want to replay. The humor should work for small kids and also make older viewers smile.

Character humor: Lumi is curious and clever and gets into funny situations; Bobo is food-loving, clumsy and easily frightened; Draco is overconfident and his magic goes funnily wrong. Every new character needs a distinct personality and their own funny moment.

**Why:** the user said "Comedy is a major part of Little Moon Story World" after ep08, which (like ep05–ep07) was a fairly serious quest structure: find the guardian, then free the magic object, then restore the kingdom.

**How to apply:** plan the gags in the script before writing code. Time the standout gag as setup, pause, payoff, with a matching sound effect. Never joke during the big emotional or restoration beat. Don't force a joke into every scene. Keep it wholesome and original.
