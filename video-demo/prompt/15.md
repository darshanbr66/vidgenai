## 1. STORY AND CONTINUITY

Episode title: The Secret of the Floating Flower Garden.

Episode 14 ended when the restored Magical Music Box projected an image of a mysterious garden where giant glowing flowers float above a lake.

Episode 15 must begin from that exact moment.

Lumi, Bobo, Draco, and Kiko follow the magical projection.

They discover an enormous floating flower garden above a sparkling crystal lake.

The garden is beautiful, but something is wrong:
- Giant flowers are slowly closing.
- Glowing petals are losing their colour.
- Floating flower islands are drifting apart.
- Butterflies are disappearing.
- The lake has stopped producing its magical sparkling waves.

The friends discover that the garden's magical Bloom Crystal has lost its power.

They must restore it before the entire floating garden disappears.

## 3. STORY SCENES

### Scene 1 – The Floating Flower Garden

- Begin with the image projected by the Magical Music Box.
- Lumi and friends arrive at the floating garden.
- Show giant glowing flowers, crystal waterfalls, floating islands, colourful butterflies, and a beautiful lake.

### Scene 3 – The Garden of Giant Flowers

- Petal guides the friends through several floating flower islands.
- Each island contains a different magical flower.
- The friends discover glowing pollen orbs scattered throughout the garden.
- Bobo mistakes a giant flower bud for a banana.
- Kiko secretly places a petal on Draco's head.
- Draco walks around thinking he has become a magical flower wizard.
- Petal's sneezes repeatedly send everyone floating through the air.

Funny moment:

Draco says, "Behold! I am the Flower Dragon!"
A flower suddenly lands on his nose and he sneezes.

### Scene 4 – The Missing Bloom Crystal

- The friends discover that the Bloom Crystal is hidden beneath the crystal lake.
- A magical flower gate protects the underwater entrance.
- The gate opens only when the correct glowing pollen orbs are placed in the right order.
- Lumi studies the flower patterns and solves the puzzle.
- Kiko tries to help but accidentally activates a giant flower.
- The flower opens and launches Bobo into a soft petal cloud.
- Draco tries to use his fire to activate another flower, but it immediately closes.

Funny moment:

Bobo shouts, "I came to save a garden, not become gardening equipment!"

### Scene 5 – Restoring the Bloom Crystal

- The friends reach the Bloom Crystal chamber beneath the lake.
- The crystal is almost completely dark.
- Petal becomes worried because she believes she has lost her magical ability.
- Lumi encourages her to believe in herself.
- Bobo, Draco, and Kiko work together to position the glowing pollen orbs.
- Petal takes a deep breath and creates a beautiful magical flower.
- The flower releases a powerful stream of colourful light.
- The Bloom Crystal begins glowing again.
- The entire floating garden starts blooming.

Funny moment:

A giant flower suddenly opens beneath Bobo and launches him gently into the air.
Bobo says, "I think the flowers are trying to eat me!"

### Scene 6 – The Garden Celebration

- The garden becomes brighter and more colourful than ever.
- Giant flowers open.
- Butterflies return.
- Magical waterfalls begin flowing.
- The lake sparkles with colourful reflections.
- Petal celebrates with the friends.
- Kiko hides inside a giant flower and surprises everyone.
- Draco tries to perform a heroic landing but lands in a pile of soft petals.
- Bobo discovers a magical golden banana-shaped flower.
- He tries to pick it, but the flower gently closes around his paw.

Everyone laughs.

End with the restored Bloom Crystal projecting an image of a mysterious magical island surrounded by glowing stars and floating lanterns.

Lumi says:

"An island in the stars? I wonder what magic is waiting there!"

End with an exciting teaser card:

"Next: The Secret of the Starlight Island"
